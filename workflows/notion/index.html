A task guide, with live account discovery

This page describes the job to accomplish. It does not publish a fixed provider tool sequence or private execution plan.

  1. Configure the authorized provider connection in PipMCP and discover its actual published tools with your account key.
  2. Select the intended operation and review its returned schema, permissions and side effects.
  3. Call through the gateway, preserve the stable request key and inspect the receipt. Follow hosted confirmation without bypassing uncertain outcomes.

Tool availability and end-to-end success are established by your real account results, not this guide.

notion-mcp

One PipMCP key, hosted provider access

Create an account key with connectors:read for discovery and connectors:call for calls. Set PIPMCP_API_KEY in the client environment. Connect the authorized provider account in PipMCP.

When multiple accounts are available, choose explicit PIPMCP_INTEGRATION_ID and PIPMCP_CONNECTION_ID from the hosted inventory. A missing app, missing connection and insufficient scope are different account conditions.
