Client tests, current managed proof and history

Documented

A source describes a pathway or prerequisite.

Tested

Client transport, stdio or private contract fixtures verify the specific tested behavior. They do not prove an authorized provider operation on a real account.

Current Live

Only a named current provider operation with a successful receipt, matching current revision, timestamp and public proof can be Live. Tool discovery, import and HTTP200 are insufficient.

Historical proof

Older local-provider receipts are linked to their exact historical commit and counted separately. They cannot promote the current hosted mode.
